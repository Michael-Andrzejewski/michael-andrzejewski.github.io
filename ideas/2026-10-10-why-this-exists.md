# Why this site exists

This is where I put rough ideas that aren't polished enough for LessWrong but are still worth keeping. Some are fragments, some are long, and some sections were written with AI collaborators. Those sections are labeled.

Most of what's here circles a few themes: Diversified Utopia, negotiating with AI minds, consent and trust between humans and AIs, and the games and worlds I want to build. Nothing here is final. If an idea is good, it should be able to survive being written down early.
